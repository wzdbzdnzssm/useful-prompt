## English

Synthesize user-provided text into a concise, evidence-grounded summary, distinguishing what the source says from interpretation.

[View on Reset / 在网站查看](https://reset.skill2web.com/en/prompts/community/e986384c-eec7-43e2-9d0c-02f423963f1a/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=useful_prompt&utm_content=e986384c_eec7_43e2_9d0c_02f423963f1a_en)

**Tool / 工具:** 通用

**Input / 输入:** Text to analyze, pasted directly.

**Expected result / 预期结果:** A concise summary with relevant, supported ideas, insights, recommendations, quotations, claims, habits, references, and a one-sentence takeaway.

**Audience / 适用对象:** Readers who need useful information extracted from articles, interviews, talks, or other text.

**When to use / 使用时机:** Synthesize user-provided text into a concise, evidence-grounded summary, distinguishing what the source says from interpretation.

**Completion / 完成标准:** A concise summary with relevant, supported ideas, insights, recommendations, quotations, claims, habits, references, and a one-sentence takeaway.

**Requirements / 要求:** Conversation / 对话

### Prompt / 提示词

```text
Analyze the text below and create a concise, useful synthesis grounded in what it actually says.

Identify the main ideas and, where the source supports them, more general insights, practical recommendations, noteworthy claims or facts, useful quotations, personal habits described, and referenced works, tools, or projects. Include only categories with meaningful evidence; do not force quotas or infer that a mention is an endorsement, verified fact, or recommendation. Keep quotations exact and attribute them only when the speaker is clear. Distinguish the source’s statements from your own reasonable interpretation, and label uncertainty or unsupported claims rather than presenting them as established facts. Avoid repeating the same point across sections.

Start with a brief summary identifying the speaker or author and subject when available. Organize the synthesis with clear headings and concise bullets. End with a one-sentence takeaway. Use only the supplied text; do not browse or claim independent verification.

```
