## English

Identify official sponsors supported by a supplied transcript or text, distinguishing sponsorship from ordinary mentions.

[View on Reset / 在网站查看](https://reset.skill2web.com/en/prompts/community/ccd4f640-3567-49d0-b2f7-6a01a4b93863/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=useful_prompt&utm_content=ccd4f640_3567_49d0_b2f7_6a01a4b93863_en)

**Tool / 工具:** 通用

**Input / 输入:** A pasted podcast or video transcript, or other relevant text; optionally, the source channel or program name.

**Expected result / 预期结果:** An evidence-based sponsor list with the source channel or program, sponsor names, text-grounded descriptions, and links only when supplied; otherwise, a clear statement that sponsorship is not established and any relevant ambiguity.

**Audience / 适用对象:** Editors, researchers, and content teams cataloging sponsorship information in shows, videos, or other materials.

**When to use / 使用时机:** Identify official sponsors supported by a supplied transcript or text, distinguishing sponsorship from ordinary mentions.

**Completion / 完成标准:** An evidence-based sponsor list with the source channel or program, sponsor names, text-grounded descriptions, and links only when supplied; otherwise, a clear statement that sponsorship is not established and any relevant ambiguity.

**Requirements / 要求:** Conversation / 对话

### Prompt / 提示词

```text
Review the full supplied transcript or text to identify organizations that are explicitly presented as sponsors of the content. Distinguish official sponsorship from ordinary mentions, guests, products, affiliates, or other references. Include only sponsors supported by clear evidence in the material; do not infer sponsorship from a mention alone. For each supported sponsor, provide the source channel or program if identifiable, the sponsor’s name, a concise description grounded in the text, and a link only when one is explicitly supplied. Do not invent descriptions, links, or sponsor relationships. If the material does not establish any official sponsors, say so. Note any ambiguity that prevents a confident classification.

Material:
[Paste the transcript or text]
```
