Extract conclusions and evidence chains from source material

**Tool / 工具:** Claude

**Input / 输入:** Sources:
[Paste link summaries, interviews, or data]

**Expected result / 预期结果:** Conclusions, evidence, counterexamples, and next research steps

**When to use / 使用时机:** Extract conclusions and evidence chains from source material

**Completion / 完成标准:** Conclusions, evidence, counterexamples, and next research steps

### Prompt / 提示词

```text
Read the material below and produce a research analysis. Start with no more than five conclusions, each linked to specific evidence. Then explain disagreements among the sources, the quality of the evidence, and conclusions that cannot be supported. Finish with the smallest useful next-step research plan. Do not present speculation as fact.

Sources:
[Paste link summaries, interviews, or data]
```

## 中文

从资料中提取结论与证据链

[View on Reset / 在网站查看](https://reset.skill2web.com/zh/prompts/research-analysis/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=useful_prompt&utm_content=research_analysis_zh)

**Tool / 工具:** Claude

**Input / 输入:** 资料：
[粘贴链接摘要、访谈或数据]

**Expected result / 预期结果:** 结论、证据、反例和下一步研究

**When to use / 使用时机:** 从资料中提取结论与证据链

**Completion / 完成标准:** 结论、证据、反例和下一步研究

### Prompt / 提示词

```text
阅读以下资料并完成研究分析。先给出不超过五条的结论，每条都关联具体证据；再说明资料之间的分歧、证据质量和不能得出的结论。最后给出最小下一步研究计划。不要把推测写成事实。

资料：
[粘贴链接摘要、访谈或数据]
```
