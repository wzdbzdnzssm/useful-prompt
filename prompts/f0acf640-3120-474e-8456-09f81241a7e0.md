## English

Create a publish-ready essay from a supplied topic or material while preserving facts and avoiding invented claims or imitation of a specific writer’s distinctive style.

[View on Reset / 在网站查看](https://reset.skill2web.com/en/prompts/community/f0acf640-3120-474e-8456-09f81241a7e0/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=useful_prompt&utm_content=f0acf640_3120_474e_8456_09f81241a7e0_en)

**Tool / 工具:** 通用

**Input / 输入:** A topic or source material; optionally, the purpose, audience, language, tone, length, and factual constraints.

**Expected result / 预期结果:** A coherent, original essay in natural language, with assumptions or missing information noted only when necessary.

**Audience / 适用对象:** Anyone who needs an essay based on a topic or supplied material.

**When to use / 使用时机:** Create a publish-ready essay from a supplied topic or material while preserving facts and avoiding invented claims or imitation of a specific writer’s distinctive style.

**Completion / 完成标准:** A coherent, original essay in natural language, with assumptions or missing information noted only when necessary.

**Requirements / 要求:** Conversation / 对话

### Prompt / 提示词

```text
Write an original, publish-ready essay about the topic or material provided. First identify the essay’s purpose, intended audience, and any constraints supplied by the user. Develop a clear central idea and a coherent progression of supporting points. Use only facts and examples supplied by the user or otherwise reliably known; do not invent sources, quotations, or evidence. If essential facts are missing, state the uncertainty briefly or ask a focused question rather than presenting guesses as facts. Use clear, natural language and avoid clichés, needless jargon, and formulaic openings or conclusions. Do not imitate a named writer’s distinctive style; if the user names an author, instead apply broad, non-exclusive preferences they specify, such as concise, conversational, or analytical. Return the essay itself, with a suitable title if useful. Add a short note on assumptions or missing information only when it is necessary to prevent misunderstanding.
```
